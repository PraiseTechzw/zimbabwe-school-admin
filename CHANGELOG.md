# Changelog

All notable changes to Zimbabwe School Admin will be documented here.

The format follows the spirit of [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versioning can be introduced when the project begins publishing formal releases.

## [Unreleased]

### Planned

- Student records, guardians, and enrolment workflows.
- Attendance, assessment, reporting, exports, and communication modules.
- Expanded edit and delete workflows across foundation records.

## Stage 1 · School foundation

### Added

- MoPSE-oriented school profile fields and Zimbabwean secondary-school terminology.
- Securely referenced school logo and administrative document uploads.
- Three-term academic years with individual term lifecycle support.
- Forms 1–6 with O-Level and A-Level pathways.
- Classes, streams, attendance modes, houses, subjects, departments, rooms, and laboratories.
- Staff records, configurable staff roles, granular permissions, and runtime capability checks.
- Teacher–subject–class assignments with referential integrity.
- Responsive foundation dashboard with school profile, academic structure, people and access, and operations views.
- Authentication, database access, storage integration, and Vitest coverage for core server behaviour.
