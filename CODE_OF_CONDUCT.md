# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Zimbabwe School Admin to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming project for contributors, maintainers, educators, administrators, and community members.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should communicate with professionalism and empathy. Give feedback about ideas and implementation rather than people. Assume good intent, explain disagreements clearly, welcome different levels of experience, and protect the privacy of schools, staff, learners, and families discussed in project work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of private or sensitive information are not acceptable. Do not upload real educational records, credentials, or confidential school documents to the repository or its issue tracker.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when behaviour violates this code. Reports should be made privately through the security or support contact described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md).

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Please include links, dates, context, and any relevant evidence, while avoiding unnecessary personal information.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, issue trackers, pull requests, community discussions, and when someone is representing the project in public.
